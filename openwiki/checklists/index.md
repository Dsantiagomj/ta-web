# Files

- [Checklist data and import](checklist-data.md) - The checklists collection (steps or souls kind, state and Squarespace link, index card, steps with a stable stepId arranged in panel tabs, text segments with stops, resolves and guide callouts, Necesitarás rows with alternatives, prerequisites with locked keys, quests that finish on their own), its validation, and how pnpm import:checklists brings over the Silvestre, Hielos, Nebuloso and Ocre without undoing panel edits.
- [Checklist page](checklist-page.md) - How /<slug> renders a published steps checklist (route order, the cached loader with the checklists tag, draft preview, the redirect of a checklist «En preparación»), the server-rendered window with Torre's route lines inside each quest, the totals in «Objetos», the details endpoint the island fetches after paint, and the island with its lazily loaded progress UI.
- [Checklists index](checklists-index.md) - How /checklists renders every checklist as a row of posters (art from each checklist's index card, ink color checked for contrast, the «???» teaser from the checklists-index global), the one cached read it shares with the menu, and the island that tilts posters and loads the progress engine and the poster progress endpoint only when the browser has saved progress.
- [Checklist progress engine](progress.md) - How checklist progress lives in the browser under one ta-web:progress key, the pure engine that decides which quests and steps are done (generic rows, quests the game finishes on its own, stops a later step finishes) and computes the weighted percentage, souls progress for the Ocre, how weights and quest relations are derived from stored quest data, and the one-time union migration of old silvestre-checklist keys.
- [Souls checklist (Ocre)](souls-checklist.md) - How a souls checklist such as the Dofus Ocre works - no stored rows, every soul derived from the bring objectives of one quest and its monsters (level, boss or archmonster, dungeon, subarea, region, stage), the server-rendered SoulsView grouped by stage or region, the level-aware search, and the island that ticks souls into ta-web:progress.
