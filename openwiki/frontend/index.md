# Files

- [Component catalog (/sistema)](component-catalog.md) - The /sistema page that renders every DESIGN.md component with real content in the five themes and both modes, the per-request gate that keeps it out of production, and the Playwright and axe checks that run against it.
- [Design system components](design-system-components.md) - The generic UI components in src/components/ui and the quest guide components in src/components/game, how they are styled, the single PageEnhancements client island that copies coordinates, plays videos and opens images fullscreen, and the pure helpers in src/lib/guides they rely on.
- [Public quest guide page](guide-page.md) - How a published quest guide renders at /<slug> (guide page v5) - loading and caching the guide, its quest, the quest giver, the next quest and its achievement page, turning the stored body into the breadcrumb, header, introduction, steps, closing and next quest card, draft preview from the panel, and revalidation on save.
- [Home page](home-page.md) - The home page at / - its sections, the counts it shares with the header, Torre's Twitch live state from his worker, the hero quest search island that loads its engine and index on first focus, the static art pipeline, and how it stays inside its JavaScript and render-blocking CSS budget.
- [Site header, footer and search](site-shell-and-search.md) - The header and footer every public page renders, what they link (the app's own pages and the legacy Squarespace site), the header client island with its menu panels, phone sheet and search dialog, the shared search engine for quests, achievements, metalogros and menu entries, and the /api/search/guides index that both searches load on first use.
- [Styles, tokens and site shell](styles-and-shell.md) - The Tailwind v4 token system in src/styles/theme.css, how theme and mode variables cascade, the self-hosted fonts, global base styles, and the current frontend layout, home page and Spanish string modules.
- [Theme and mode](theme-and-mode.md) - The two appearance settings, the theme (four free themes with Amakna as default, Ocre premium and entitlement-checked) and the dark or light mode, both kept in a cookie and localStorage and rendered by the server, plus the pre-paint script, the header theme picker, the ModeToggle island and the browser bar color.
- [Zone background](zone-background.md) - The landscape of a quest guide's or achievement page's zone, fixed behind the whole page under a flat veil. Where the image comes from (the page's own upload or its subarea's, a cropped DofusDB map render), how the panel button and pnpm zones:backgrounds fill it, how pages read it inside their cached read, and the CSS that keeps text and rules readable over it.
