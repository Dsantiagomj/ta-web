- [Analytics with Umami](analytics.md) - How ta-web counts page views with a self-hosted Umami through its own small inline script, without cookies or personal data, how two environment variables turn it on in production only, and how the tests keep it off or point it at a fake Umami.
- [Deployment and releases](deployment.md) - How ta-web reaches Railway, from the infrastructure-as-code service definition through dev auto-deploys with a post-deploy smoke to the owner-only production release workflow.

- [Health and readiness endpoints](health-check.md) - The public GET /api/health liveness endpoint, which never touches the database, and GET /api/ready, which pings Postgres with bounded time and without poisoning the pool; their response contracts, the single-flight database check, and who calls each.
- [Legacy link reports and URL map](legacy-links.md) - The two read-only reports for the domain cutover - pnpm urls:map, what this site will do with every old Squarespace URL, and pnpm links:legacy, every link the site would still render to Squarespace - with the shared blocker groups, the typo detection and how they are run.
- [Legacy redirects, sitemap and robots.txt](redirects-and-seo.md) - How old Squarespace URLs are answered with a 301, 307 or 410 by src/proxy.ts from one validated table, how the generated stubs section is rebuilt, what /sitemap.xml lists and how it is cached, and what robots.txt says in each environment.
- [Share cards, JSON-LD and update dates](share-cards-and-structured-data.md) - How each page gets its own share image drawn on the server with next/og, which Article and WebSite JSON-LD the site emits, and how contentUpdatedAt records when Torre last published a content change for the «Actualizado el» line and dateModified.
